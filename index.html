Step 3 of 7 · Who can Lemonade win?

How fit and openness are scored

Carriers are profitable again (2025 combined ratio 91.8%) and premium growth has stalled (−0.6% in 2026): expect them to fight harder for good customers.

Opportunity = carrier's premium in play in Lemonade Car's states (share × Car-state premium × 53% shopped × 48% online) × Fit × Openness. Ticked rows add up to the “winnable” figure. Fit = their customers look like Lemonade's. Openness = 2023 rate hike + weak agent/home-bundle ties. Scores 1–5 are editable estimates.

1The promise
Landing page
The insight
Drivers want a price that reflects them, and big insurers rarely say so.
How I'd say it
  • Car insurance priced on you, not everyone else.
What I'd expect
More visitors start a quote.
2The proof
Quote flow
The insight
The flow asks for miles without explaining why they matter.
How I'd say it
  • Welcome, Jordana. A few questions so we can tailor your quote.
  • We see your Lemonade Homeowners policy. Any other policies with another company?
  • How far do you drive in a year? Your price is built on your miles, not the national average.
  • Based on your answers, your Lemonade Car coverage also includes…
  • Adding up your miles… Checking your car’s safety features… Charging up
  • Hey Jordana, here’s the quote we built for you! We based it on: your 2024 RAV4 Hybrid, your 8,000 miles a year, and how nicely you drive ;-)
What I'd expect
More people finish the quote and buy.
3The exits
When there's no online quote
The insight
A dead end with no reason can lose someone who arrived interested.
How I'd say it
  • Lemonade Car is coming to Connecticut. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you the day it arrives. Need coverage today? Our partner Bristol West can quote you now.
  • This one needs a human touch. A licensed agent can help: 1-844-942-3866.
What I'd expect
More people take a next step when they hit a roadblock: improved dead-end recovery rate.
The outcome
More of the people who land on the page end up with a policy.
